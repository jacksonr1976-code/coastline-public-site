Pool Service FAQs

What is included with weekly pool service?

Coastline’s weekly service includes cleaning, water treatment, brushing, vacuuming and equipment checks. Contact Coastline to discuss the needs of your pool.

Which areas does Coastline Pools serve?

Coastline lists Murrells Inlet, Garden City Beach, Surfside Beach, Myrtle Beach, Carolina Forest, Pawleys Island and Georgetown, South Carolina. Contact Coastline to confirm that your address fits the current route.

Can Coastline help with a green or algae-affected pool?

Yes. Coastline offers green pool recovery and can discuss the condition of your pool and the next service step.

Does Coastline Pools handle pool equipment repairs?

Coastline lists pool and equipment repair, including help with pumps, filters and plumbing. Describe the issue when you call, text or request a quote.

How do I request a pool service quote?

Use this quote form, call (843) 999-0171 or text Coastline at (843) 999-0171. Include your location and the service you need so Coastline can follow up.
